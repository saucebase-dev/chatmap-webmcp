# Wayfinder

Wayfinder helps people explore places through a guided conversation and a map.

## Language

**Map-ready plan**:
The saved, structured understanding of a visitor's intent and preferences before map exploration begins.
_Avoid_: Search plan, itinerary, chat summary

**Plan review card**:
The expandable card centred over the map staging panel through which a visitor reviews a map-ready plan before opening the map.
_Avoid_: Summary message, itinerary card

**Map staging panel**:
The right-side area that shows a blurred map behind the discovery interview or plan review card until map exploration begins.
_Avoid_: Empty map, preview map

**Onboarding phase**:
A conversation's current stage: discovery interview, plan review, or map exploration.
_Avoid_: Screen state, chat mode

**Discovery interview**:
A short guided conversation that collects the information needed to create a map-ready plan.
_Avoid_: Questionnaire, intake form
